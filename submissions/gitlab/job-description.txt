An overview of this role
When a customer pushes a change, they need their CI/CD jobs to start quickly and run reliably, even when demand spikes. You'll help make that happen on GitLab.com by improving how Hosted Runners provision compute, isolate customer workloads, and recover from failures. Your decisions will shape how long customers wait for jobs to start and how efficiently GitLab uses cloud compute.

As a Senior Staff Site Reliability Engineer, you'll set technical direction across teams while continuing to write and operate production software in Go. You'll lead work across the runner control plane, worker provisioning, and job execution. Your primary focus will be GitLab.com, and you'll also provide technical leadership support for GitLab Dedicated Hosted Runners. You'll report to the engineering manager responsible for Hosted Runners.

Some examples of our projects:

- Tuning autoscaling to reduce job wait times during demand spikes

- Evaluating ways to isolate customer workloads

- Improving how runner usage is measured for capacity planning and billing

What You'll Do

- Lead architecture and delivery across teams. Plan migrations with representative workload tests, safe capacity handovers, and rollback paths.

- Write, review, and ship Go code across GitLab Runner, autoscaling components, cloud integrations, and operational tooling.

- Improve reliability from job pickup through cleanup. Join the Runners Platform on-call rotation, investigate failures across system layers, and verify fixes in production.

- Work with Security and Runner engineers to evaluate workload isolation, balancing security and compatibility with performance and cost.

- Build observability that connects the customer experience with fleet behavior and guides capacity decisions. Partner with Finance, Product, and the billing platform team on usage metering and cost attribution.

- Reduce recurring operational work through automation, tooling, and runbooks that engineers can use independently.

- Mentor experienced engineers through design, implementation, and operations. Clarify ownership across teams and help engineers validate AI-assisted changes before they reach production.

What You'll Bring

- Recent hands-on experience building and operating production systems software such as controllers, agents, or schedulers. You've personally shipped code that handles challenges such as concurrency, retries, or resource cleanup.

- Strong programming skills in Go or another systems language, with the ability to become productive in Go.

- Deep Linux and networking knowledge. You can trace production failures across system layers and use measurements to validate fixes.

- Experience operating Kubernetes in production, including diagnosing scheduling and resource issues and managing rollouts and recovery.

- Experience building or operating autoscaled or dynamically scheduled compute, such as VM fleets, serverless platforms, or batch systems. You bring experience with public cloud and infrastructure automation or comparable fleet operations.

- A working understanding of the CI job lifecycle and the implications of running customer-controlled code. You can bring that knowledge from outside a CI/CD team.

- A record of leading technical initiatives across teams and personally contributing to implementation through production. You build agreement through clear writing and asynchronous collaboration, and mentor engineers to own what they build and operate.

About the team
Hosted Runners operates the compute behind customers' CI/CD jobs. You'll work with Runner Core to turn lessons from operating the fleet into improvements to the open-source GitLab Runner. You'll also work with Product and partner teams to agree on priorities and ownership for shared changes.
