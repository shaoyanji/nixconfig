Meet the team behind this journey
You will join the team building and operating DeepL for Sovereign Markets, our offering for public sector bodies and regulated organisations that need more than data residency. It is hosted and operated entirely within the EU, under EU jurisdiction, and runs alongside DeepL's global infrastructure. The team is designing the platform foundations that make that promise true in practice: how services are built, deployed, secured, observed and operated. The approach favours managed infrastructure and well designed software abstractions over bespoke low level plumbing. You will work closely with product, security and legal colleagues as the offering grows to cover more capabilities and features.
Your responsibilities
Build the platform as software:
Design and build the platform layer of DeepL for Sovereign Markets as software: reusable services, abstractions and tooling that product engineers build on
Own the architecture:
Own the architecture of the managed infrastructure the platform runs on, including Kubernetes, networking, storage, identity and secrets, using provider managed services where they meet the requirement
Standardise and automate:
Define infrastructure as code and delivery standards that hold across teams, and automate operations so that manual work is the exception
Make design decisions visible and reviewable:
write and review architecture decision records, weigh positions on their merits and commit once a decision is made
Build for reliability:
Build observability, resilience testing and incident response practices so that the service meets its reliability commitments
Adapt the product to the new environment:
Make low invasive modifications to existing code in the product with confidence in order to make it run in the new environment
Keep the environment auditable:
Work with security and compliance colleagues to keep the environment auditable and aligned with the standards our customers require
Raise the technical bar:
Mentor engineers, raise the technical bar through design reviews, and build consensus across teams without relying on line authority
Participation in our on-call rotation:
You’ll ensure the reliability and availability of our services by being available to join the team's shared on-call rotation as needed
