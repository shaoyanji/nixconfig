Depth

層

Four levels, none of them a shadow: paper, tint wash, raised well, inverted bone. Grain is the only texture; motion is 90 / 170 / 210 ms with no bounce.

Keep it going · 支

One bone band per page. It is the loudest thing here.

07

Don'ts

禁
  • Shadows, glows, blur panels
  • Radii above 2 px, pills, chips
  • A second accent colour
  • Light mode
  • Uppercase serif, mono paragraphs
  • Zebra tables, three-stat dashboards
  • Gradients, neon, glassmorphism
  • Kanji as the only label
