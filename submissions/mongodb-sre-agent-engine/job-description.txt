Atlas Agent Engine is a platform that runs AI agents as customer workloads in strongly isolated environments, on multi-tenant Kubernetes clusters across multiple cloud providers. The platform's SRE team owns the operational foundations: the Kubernetes fleet, networking, observability and alerting, and tenant isolation.

As part of an expanding SRE team, you will play a key role in defining operational strategies for our runtime, enhancing runtime security, and supporting expansion across additional cloud platforms.

We are looking to speak to candidates who are based in Dublin for our hybrid working model.

The ideal candidate should:

Have 6+ years of experience working on software development and operating distributed systems

Proficiency in Go, or a similar language

Have operated Kubernetes in production and be comfortable debugging it below the abstraction: scheduling, cluster networking, and node-level issues

Understanding of Linux operating system internals and networking concepts (e.g., TCP/IP, DNS, TLS, routing)

Expertise in cloud infrastructure platforms, including AWS, Google Cloud Platform (GCP), or Azure

Value efficiency in processes and operations

Possess a customer-focused mindset

Responsibilities

Operate and improve the multi-tenant Kubernetes infrastructure that runs customer workloads

Build for reliability, making services and infrastructure available, resilient, fault-tolerant, and self-healing

Identify and configure key metrics to detect incidents and quantify service health, availability, and performance

Participate in a 24/7 on-call rotation to resolve issues involving the platform infrastructure

Strong candidates may also have experience with

Kubernetes networking (e.g., Istio, Cilium) and service mesh or edge load balancing in production

Designing secure, multi-tenant runtime environments at scale

Managing and scaling infrastructure across multi-cloud environments (AWS, GCP, or Azure)

Virtualization and workload isolation technologies
