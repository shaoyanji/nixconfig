Senior Platform Engineer
Senior Platform Engineer
We are looking for a passionate and collaborative Senior Platform Engineer to join our Platform Core team. At Treatwell we follow Team Topologies principles: our platform exists to enable stream-aligned teams to move faster and more autonomously, through self-service capabilities, reliable infrastructure and a great developer experience, built in partnership with our Developer Experience and Data Platform teams.
The Platform Core team provides the foundational infrastructure and tooling that enables Treatwell's engineering organisation to ship value quickly and safely. As a Senior Platform Engineer you will own the ambiguous platform problems nobody has scoped yet. You will shape them with the team before they become work, design solutions at service level and make the technical calls in your domain.
We want product teams to experience Platform Core as a strategic partner: a trusted advisor involved early, not a ticket resolver. We deliver value in small increments, own outcomes together with the teams we support, and measure success by the impact we enable across Engineering
You will be responsible for:
Owning core platform capabilities (AWS and GCP foundations, identity and access, networking and the AI layer on Bedrock), from ambiguous problems to production, delivered in small slices.
Design delivery and operation of our EKS platform in partnership with DevX, including upgrades, add-ons and autoscaling.
Building self-service tooling, such as OpenTofu pipelines and GitLab CI/CD components, so teams can provision infrastructure themselves.
Running GitLab and other shared platform services: upgrades, patching, monitoring and recovery.
Making sure every platform change goes through a reviewed pipeline.
Making and owning the technical decisions in your domain, using spikes to test options and escalating early when needed.
Commanding platform incidents, and following through on post-mortems.
Improving the security of the services you own, in line with the AWS Well-Architected Framework.
Using AI tools in your daily work and helping others do the same.
Mentoring engineers, giving useful feedback.
Shaping engineering standards through our guilds
Partnering with product teams early, understanding their plans before they need us, and making Platform Core easy to work with.
Aligning teams on approach before work starts, and explaining trade-offs in terms of risk and value.
Using platform metrics and support data to argue for what to do next, and shaping new work with your team before it is defined
About you
Several years in platform, infrastructure, with clear ownership of production platforms used by other engineering teams.
Deep hands-on Terraform (or OpenTofu) on AWS, including module design and multi-account or multi-tenant setups.
Expert with Kubernetes (EKS) and containers; experience running cluster upgrades and Helm or Kustomize at scale.
Strong in GitLab CI and designing reusable pipelines and templates for many teams.
Professional depth in core AWS (EC2, VPC, IAM, Route53, CloudFront, S3, RDS, EKS). GCP exposure is a bonus.
Experience with, or strong interest in, modern cloud networking such as AWS VPC Lattice and service mesh with support for identity-based access between services
Fluent in Bash and Python for automation; comfortable on Linux
Practical observability experience with Datadog, Prometheus, CloudWatch or Grafana, including defining SLIs and alerting.
Security-minded: IAM least privilege, secrets management, container supply-chain security, and applying the AWS Well-Architected Framework and DSOMM.
Experience leading production incidents and making technical decisions you own, including when they turn out wrong.
Have grown other engineers and can explain complex trade-offs clearly to technical and non-technical audiences.
