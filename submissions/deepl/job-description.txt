Meet the team behind this journey
You will join the team building and operating DeepL for Sovereign Markets, our offering for public sector bodies and regulated organisations that need more than data residency. It is hosted and operated entirely within the EU, under EU jurisdiction, and runs alongside DeepL's global infrastructure. The team is designing the platform foundations that make that promise true in practice: how services are built, deployed, secured, observed and operated. The approach favours managed infrastructure and well designed software abstractions over bespoke low level plumbing. You will work closely with product, security and legal colleagues as the offering grows to cover more capabilities and features.
Your responsibilities
-   Build the platform as software:  Design and build the platform layer of DeepL for Sovereign Markets as software: reusable services, abstractions and tooling that product engineers build on
-   Own the architecture:  Own the architecture of the managed infrastructure the platform runs on, including Kubernetes, networking, storage, identity and secrets, using provider managed services where they meet the requirement
-   Standardise and automate:  Define infrastructure as code and delivery standards that hold across teams, and automate operations so that manual work is the exception
-   Make design decisions visible and reviewable:  write and review architecture decision records, weigh positions on their merits and commit once a decision is made
-   Build for reliability:  Build observability, resilience testing and incident response practices so that the service meets its reliability commitments
-   Adapt the product to the new environment:  Make low invasive modifications to existing code in the product with confidence in order to make it run in the new environment
-   Keep the environment auditable:  Work with security and compliance colleagues to keep the environment auditable and aligned with the standards our customers require
-   Raise the technical bar:  Mentor engineers, raise the technical bar through design reviews, and build consensus across teams without relying on line authority
-   Participation in our on-call rotation:  You’ll ensure the reliability and availability of our services by being available to join the team's shared on-call rotation as needed
Qualities we look for
-   Software engineering depth:  Strong software engineering skills in at least one major language, C#, Go or Python preferred, shown by tooling, services or platform abstractions that other engineers rely on
-   Production infrastructure:  Production experience designing and running managed infrastructure and Kubernetes, with sound judgement on when to use provider managed services and when not to
-   Automation and delivery:  Infrastructure as code with Terraform, Crossplane or equivalent, and GitOps delivery, applied to environments you have owned
-   AI-Native Velocity:  You leverage AI-powered tools (like GitHub Copilot or Claude) to accelerate your velocity and eliminate repetitive toil. You use AI for boilerplate code, test generation, and debugging, allowing you to focus your cognitive energy on high-value architectural decisions and complex problem-solving
-   A track record of technical ownership across several teams:  you create clarity where a mandate crosses team boundaries, hold clear positions and change them on evidence
-   Reliability discipline:  observability, incident response and designing for failure, with experience carrying on-call
-   Security fundamentals:  Working knowledge of infrastructure security fundamentals, such as identity, secrets, network segmentation and supply chain hygiene
-   Clear written communication:  you can capture a decision so that others can build on it
You’d get bonus points for
-  Experience in regulated or audited environments, for example BSI C5, ISO 27001 or SOC2
-  Experience with EU based or sovereign cloud providers
-  Chaos engineering and game day practice
-  Building platforms for small teams through a high degree of automation
