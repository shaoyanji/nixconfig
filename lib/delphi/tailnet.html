03 What this page promises

Every card above is declared in services.servicePortal — a tailscale serve mount, or a daemon bound to the tailnet address — and the page is compiled from that declaration: add one and its card appears, remove it and the card goes. The build fails instead of drifting when a mount has no card, two mounts share a port, an upstream is not loopback, or a requires names a unit that does not exist on this host.

It shows structure, not liveness. A card says where a service is, never that it is up — those are different claims, and the monitors on the tailnet are the ones allowed to make the second. Nothing here is sampled from a running process, so nothing here can be stale in a way that looks live.
