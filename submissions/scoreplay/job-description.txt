ScorePlay is the AI-powered media infrastructure for sports, built to automate the content operations behind the world’s biggest sports organizations. We work with 350+ leagues, competitions, teams and broadcasters – including NBA and NFL teams, Premier League, LaLiga and Ligue 1 clubs, leading bodies like UEFA and FIBA, the Tour de France, and the Australian Open – helping them turn every moment on and off the field into high-value, structured content. Our platform brings all their media into one place and makes it effortless to find, produce and share in seconds.

Founded in 2021, we’re a 65-person team based between New York, Paris, and 10+ other countries, growing 2x year over year with 97% retention. We’ve raised $20M+ from top investors including Alexis Ohanian and Harry Stebbing’s 20VC and elite athletes like Kevin Durant, Raphaël Varane, Mario Götze, Nico Rosberg and Giannis Antetokounmpo.

If you thrive in fast-paced environments and you want to help shape how the next generation of fans experience sports, you’re in the right place. We’re on track to become the global leader in sports media, and this is definitely the best time to join our adventure. 


⚙️ THE ROLE

ScorePlay is the platform major sports organizations use to manage, search and share their media, from the second it's captured to the moment it reaches everyone's screen. A photographer uploads thousands of shots after the final whistle. A live feed has to be logged and clipped into highlights while the game is still being played. The right assets have to reach 10,000+ athletes, partners and newsrooms worldwide, automatically, not one file at a time.

Underneath that sits 15+ petabytes of media, 145M+ minutes of video ingested every month, a multi-region distributed architecture, and event-driven pipelines that have to hold up on match day. Much of the infrastructure needed at that scale simply hasn't been built before.

All of it runs on infrastructure built and operated by our Platform team, which today is one person. You'll be the second.

You'll work hand in hand with Maxime https://www.linkedin.com/in/maximerenou50/, our founding SRE, who built this platform from nothing and is looking for a peer, not a report. You'll split the roadmap based on your strengths and preferences, report directly to our Head of Engineering, and own topics end to end: scoping, choosing the technical approach, and shipping to production. Much of the platform exists. A lot of what comes next is still to be designed.




🔥 WHY THIS IS TECHNICALLY INTERESTING


Storage economics at 15PB and growing. Storage and egress dominate our bill, so cost is an architectural problem, not a spreadsheet exercise. It's also why we're building Bring Your Own Storage: clients keep their media in their own cloud account and our platform operates on top of it. Making media sharing economically viable across providers, at petabyte scale, without giving up performance, is not a solved problem, and it's a commercial differentiator, not a side project.

High availability when there is no maintenance window. A match happens once. Live ingest, streaming and distribution have to hold during the exact hours our clients care about most, across multiple regions. You'll own the architecture that makes match day boring.

A platform product, with engineers as the users. Product engineers should be able to develop locally, deploy through clean pipelines, and spin up the infrastructure they need without waiting on you. Turning Platform from a potential bottleneck into an enabler, self-service tooling, clear processes, fast support on incoming requests, is half the job.




🧠 WHAT YOU'LL OWN: 


Infrastructure (the core of the job)

 - Design, build and operate our AWS infrastructure across multiple regions, deployed on Kubernetes and managed entirely through Terraform, Terragrunt and Helm.

 - Own high-availability architectures, object storage at 15PB+ scale, and event streaming (Kafka) in a distributed, event-driven system.

 - Drive cost optimization across the stack, and shape multi-cloud and Bring Your Own Storage architectures that make data sharing economically viable for us and our partners.

Developer Experience

Security & Compliance

🛠 THE STACK AND HOW WE WORK

AWS, multi-region. Kubernetes, Terraform and Helm for everything. Kafka for event streaming, object storage at petabyte scale, PostgreSQL, OpenSearch, Temporal for workflows, Go for services. We use AI heavily (but wisely), in the product and in how we work as engineers.

Cross-functional feature teams own their scope from conception to production. Structural decisions go through RFCs, you write them, you defend them. Every week the whole engineering team gets together to present and debate technical subjects. Every incident gets a postmortem, with real numbers attached.

Remote. We're remote-first with regular team gatherings, an international team spread across Europe and the US.
