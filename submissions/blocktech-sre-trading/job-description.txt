BlockTech is hiring a Senior Site Reliability Engineer to join its Tech department in Amsterdam.
Crypto markets never close, and neither does our trading. Bare-metal hosts, cloud instances, exchange connectivity, messaging, and the trading services on top all have to work around the clock.
In this role, you'll sit between infrastructure, engineering, and trading, and follow every problem wherever it leads.
About BlockTech
BlockTech is an algorithmic trading firm headquartered in Amsterdam, with a second office in Singapore. We specialize in market making and trade cryptocurrency derivatives and spot markets globally, using our own capital. We're growing fast and expanding into new markets.
We trade options and delta one around the clock on the largest crypto venues. Our platform runs on a Linux bare-metal fleet alongside cloud deployments in the regions where exchanges run, with services written in Python and Rust.
What will you do?
You will make sure everything works, and works properly. Most problems don't stay inside one team's boundaries: a strange fill might come from a network issue, a config change, or an exchange that changed its behavior without telling anyone. You understand what each service does for trading, what it costs when it breaks, and which fix matters most right now. You own an issue from the first alert to the long-term fix that stops it coming back.
You will:
Own the live environment.
 Monitor trading across all our venues, take charge of incidents, and own both the immediate fix and the long-term one.
Bridge infrastructure, engineering, and trading.
 Translate a trader's “something looks off” into a root cause, and a root cause into a priority the right team can act on.
Partner with the desk.
 Work closely with traders and quants, so reliability work is driven by what actually matters for PnL and risk.
Debug across the full stack.
 Linux and network performance, exchange connectivity, messaging and databases (Kafka, NATS, Redis, PostgreSQL/TimescaleDB, ClickHouse), and the Python and Rust trading services themselves.
Own our hybrid infrastructure.
 Run the bare-metal fleet and cloud deployments, plus their configuration management and deployment pipelines across environments.
Build observability.
 Use Prometheus/VictoriaMetrics, Grafana, Sentry, and Kibana to catch a problem before the trading desk does.
Change things safely.
 Plan and run maintenance, upgrades, and releases without trading going dark.
Raise the bar.
 Cut toil with automation, and spread SRE practices across Tech so every team builds reliable systems from the start.
What we're looking for
You'll need:
5+ years running production systems where uptime and correctness matter, in an SRE, DevOps, systems, or software engineering role.
At least 2 years of that in a trading environment (a trading firm, exchange, or broker), working directly with live trading systems and traders.
A working understanding of how trading systems operate: order and quote flow, positions and PnL, exchange connectivity, and why latency and correctness matter.
A commercial mindset. You can weigh technical trade-offs against business impact and explain them clearly to traders and engineers alike.
Strong Linux skills, including troubleshooting performance at the OS and kernel level.
Solid networking fundamentals: TCP/IP, UDP, and multicast, plus comfort reading a packet capture.
Hands-on experience running production workloads in a public cloud (AWS or similar): networking (VPCs, peering, cross-region latency), compute choices, and infrastructure as code (Terraform or similar).
Proficiency in Python, and the ability to read and debug code you didn't write, including our Rust services.
Hands-on experience with configuration management (Ansible or similar) and CI/CD.
Experience operating at least some of our stack: Kafka, NATS, PostgreSQL, ClickHouse, Redis, Prometheus, or Grafana.
Calm, structured incident handling, and clear communication in English under pressure.
Bonus points for:
Experience with options or crypto markets.
Exchange connectivity experience (WebSocket or FIX APIs).
Low-latency or market-making environments.
Hands-on Rust experience. Much of our platform is written in it.
Network hardware (Arista, Cisco), routing protocols, or packet capture tooling.
Server hardware and kernel tuning for low latency.
Running data-heavy systems such as time-series databases.
