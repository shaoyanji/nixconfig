About the Role
Join a small back-office IT team at a SaaS company, helping maintain secure and reliable infrastructure across a heterogeneous Windows and Linux environment. You will support core systems and contribute to infrastructure and compliance projects.
What You'll Do
Administer and maintain servers, networks, and storage across Windows and Linux systems.
Ensure the availability of core infrastructure, including Proxmox, Kubernetes, CEPH, and networking.
Install, configure, and support hardware and software.
Monitor system performance and maintain the availability and security of IT infrastructure.
Investigate and resolve technical issues and service disruptions.
Plan and deliver IT projects, including work related to ISO 27001 and C5 compliance requirements.
What We're Looking For
At least 3 years of experience in system administration or a comparable role managing servers, networks, and storage.
Completed vocational training, a degree in computer science, or a comparable qualification.
Production experience with virtualization, particularly Proxmox, and administration of both Windows and Linux systems.
Strong knowledge of server administration, networking, and IT security.
Experience with Microsoft 365 and cloud services such as Azure in hybrid environments.
German proficiency at C1 level and English proficiency at B2 level.
Experience with Kubernetes, CEPH, procurement, supplier management, or ISO 27001/C5 compliance is beneficial.
Compensation & Benefits
An indefinite-term employment contract with compensation based on qualifications and experience. Visa sponsorship is not available.
Location
On-site in Freiburg im Breisgau, Germany.
