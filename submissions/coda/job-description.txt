Why Coda
Coda is a global growth engine for commerce, connecting people, digital products, and payments through trusted monetization and distribution solutions.
With 600+ people from 57 nationalities across 23 locations, we’re a truly global team headquartered in Singapore, with offices in Amsterdam, Dubai, Bristol, Shanghai, Eindhoven, and across Southeast Asia.
We power global commerce through a dual model. Our B2B solutions, Codapay , Coda Webstore, Coda Links, Coda Distribution, and Giftcloud , enable publishers and brands to monetize and distribute digital content globally. On the consumer side, we operate a portfolio of trusted storefronts including  Codashop , Recharge.com , Startselect.com , and mobiletopup.co.uk , that give customers secure and easy access to game top-ups, digital credits, vouchers, gift cards and prepaid products in over 70 markets.

If you’re looking to grow your career and make a real impact in a global team, Coda is the place for you.
The Opportunity
We’re looking for an experienced Cloud Infrastructure Engineer with strong AWS and cloud security expertise to join our global Infrastructure Operations team.
You’ll architect, build, secure and maintain the cloud infrastructure behind Coda’s platforms. Beyond daily operations, you’ll help us manage infrastructure better at scale, strengthen our AWS security and make changes safer and simpler for engineering teams.
You’ll work closely with engineers across the company to solve infrastructure challenges, improve reliability and cut down manual work through automation.
What You'll Do
- Design, build and maintain secure, scalable and highly available AWS infrastructure across multiple accounts and regions
- Manage infrastructure as code and improve our CI/CD pipelines for automated deployments
Strengthen AWS security with controls and policies across the organization, plus well designed IAM and network security rules
- Spot and fix security risks, misconfigurations and vulnerabilities, working closely with our Security team on reviews, investigations and remediation
- Improve monitoring, logging and alerting so teams can catch and resolve issues faster
- Automate repetitive tasks and make it easier for developers to self serve their infrastructure needs
- Keep our platforms reliable, performant and cost efficient, including taking part in on call rotations, troubleshooting production incidents and preventing them from happening again
- Collaborate with other engineering teams, share what you know and help shape technical decisions
What You'll Bring
- 5+ years in infrastructure operations, cloud, platform engineering or DevOps, with at least 3 of those years working on AWS
- Hands on experience with core AWS services like EC2, ECS, Lambda, RDS, VPC, Route 53 and CloudFront, including managing multiple accounts through AWS Organizations
- Confident with Terraform or a similar Infrastructure as Code tool, plus building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines
- Strong grasp of AWS security, including IAM, access across accounts, SCPs, RCPs and network security, along with auditing tools like CloudTrail, AWS Config, GuardDuty or Security Hub
- Solid networking knowledge, including VPC, routing, firewalls, VPN and private connectivity
- Comfortable with Linux, scripting in Python or Bash, and containers using Docker, ECS or Kubernetes
- Familiar with monitoring tools like Datadog, Grafana, Prometheus or OpenTelemetry, with strong troubleshooting skills and a collaborative way of working across engineering teams
- It’s a plus if you’ve worked with security automation tools like Cloud Custodian, Checkov or Trivy, know standards such as PCI DSS or the CIS AWS Foundations Benchmark, have experience with high traffic distributed systems or payment platforms, or hold an AWS certification like Security Specialty, Solutions Architect or Advanced Networking
