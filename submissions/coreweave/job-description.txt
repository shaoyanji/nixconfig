What You’ll Do
As a Senior Data Platform Engineer, you will apply advanced knowledge of software engineering, data architecture, distributed systems, and database technologies to design and operate our enterprise data platform. Your responsibilities will include:
- Design and implement scalable data systems and pipelines — 30%: Architect, develop, test, and maintain distributed batch and streaming pipelines that ingest, transform, validate, and deliver structured and semi-structured data across production systems.
- Develop data transformation and orchestration frameworks — 20%: Build and optimize workflows using technologies such as dbt, Dagster, Airflow, or comparable tools. Establish testing, deployment, dependency-management, data-quality, and observability standards for these workflows.
- Improve data warehouse performance and reliability — 15%: Design data models, schemas, partitioning strategies, and storage patterns for large-scale analytical workloads. Diagnose and resolve performance, scalability, availability, and cost-efficiency issues within multi-petabyte data environments.
- Build product telemetry and data instrumentation — 15%: Partner with software engineering teams to define event schemas and implement reliable logging, telemetry, and instrumentation for web applications, backend services, and distributed systems.
- Develop data catalog and semantic-layer capabilities — 10%: Design metadata, lineage, governance, and semantic-modeling systems that enable consistent business definitions and secure self-service analytics.
- Support data-powered AI applications — 5%: Establish data interfaces and platform foundations for internal AI applications, including systems that support automated data discovery, workflow assistance, customer-growth analysis, churn-risk modeling, and abuse detection.
- Operate and support the platform — 5%: Monitor production systems, troubleshoot incidents, document operational procedures, and participate in an on-call rotation after completing the required onboarding and training.
This position requires independent technical judgment to evaluate architectural alternatives, identify system constraints, and select appropriate data structures, algorithms, database designs, and distributed-processing patterns.

- Professional experience designing and implementing production data pipelines and distributed data-processing systems.
- Professional experience with data transformation or workflow-orchestration technologies such as dbt, Dagster, Airflow, or comparable platforms.
- Professional experience designing data models, schemas, and storage strategies for large-scale analytical databases or cloud data warehouses.
