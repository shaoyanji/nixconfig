HPC Systems Engineer – Machine Learning Infrastructure & Operations (m/f/d, E13 TV-L, 100%)
The Cluster of Excellence "Machine Learning - New Perspectives for Science" at the University of Tübingen offers a position as
HPC Systems Engineer – Machine Learning Infrastructure & Operations (m/f/d, E13 TV-L, 100%)
The position is available in the team of the Machine Learning Science Cloud and is permanent.
About Us
The ML Cloud team designs, runs and safeguards high-performance computing infrastructure tailored to machine learning workloads. Our state of the art systems are distributed across four data centers and are operated for the Tübingen AI Center, the Cluster of Excellence ’Machine Learning: New Perspectives for Science’ and the Hertie Institute for AI in Brain Health. Every day, researchers unleash thousands of compute jobs on our systems, training frontier-scale neural networks and running experiments.
The Opportunity
Our clusters keep growing – more GPUs, more storage, and more services around them. We are looking for an HPC Systems Eingineer who enjoys developing and running production infrastructure end to end: from bare-metal compute and the Slurm scheduler to the virtualization layer on which central service run, the storage backends and the automation that holds it all together. You will keep our systems fast and reliable, extend them as demand grows, and turn manual work into reproducible, code-driven operations that the whole team can rely on. You will be joining a motivated team of HPC specialists who share responsibility for the whole stack, work closely, and have real room to shape how our infrastructure is built and run. You will also have the opportunity to shape two new directions: AI agents in our administrative workflows, and agent-native access to the clusters for our users.
Your Tasks
Design and operate HPC clusters across four data centers – spanning inter-/intra-site networks, SLURM scheduling, parallel filesystems (e.g., Lustre/Ceph), and GPU accelerators – ensuring high availability and sustained throughput for research workloads.
Build and manage provisioning and configuration of heterogeneous compute, storage and network nodes (e.g. image-based provisioning, node lifecycle, Layer 2 and Layer 3 networking)
Run and extend our Proxmox virtualization clusters hosting the management plane and infrastructure services (HA, live migration, backup)
Operate and scale our storage platforms: Ceph clusters for S3-compatible object storage for research data, including capacity planning, tiering, replication and backup
Automate operations as code with Ansible and Git-based workflows – provisioning, configuration, roll-outs and recurring maintenance
Build and operate monitoring, logging and alerting, and keep systems patched, hardened and reliable through regular maintenance windows
Plan and carry out hardware roll-outs, capacity extensions and migrations together with vendors and data center staff
Bring AI agents into our admin workflows and build agent-native access to the cluster for users.
Support and advise users on efficient cluster usage (job scheduling, data handling, storage layout, access workflows)
Your profile
Masters degree in Computer Science or a related field
Solid Linux administration experience in a production environment (RHEL/Rocky/Ubuntu)
Hands-on HPC experience: Slurm, parallel file systems (Weka, Lustre, Ceph), GPU workloads (driver/CUDA, DCGM, Fabric Manager, NVLink/NVSwitch, MIG) and high-speed networks (InfiniBand, 400G Ethernet)
Solid data-center networking skills: Ethernet/IP (VLANs, routing, bonding/LACP), InfiniBand fabric management (subnet manager, fabric diagnostics), and firewall/VPN configuration
Experience operating Ceph and S3-compatible object storage (e.g. RADOS Gateway) at scale
Experience with virtualization for the management plane and infrastructure services (Proxmox, including clustering and backup)
Strong automation skills with Ansible (roles, inventories, CI-driven roll-outs) and scripting in Bash and Python
containers (Apptainer/Singularity/Docker), monitoring stacks (Prometheus/Grafana), or database and service operations
A plus: Hands-on LLM tooling (tool calling and APIs, agent frameworks, retrieval and evaluation)
Independent, structured working style and good communication in English; German is a plus
A collaborative, user-facing mindset – comfortable supporting and advising researchers
What We Offer
Technically deep, architecturally open work that directly enables cutting-edge machine learning research
A small, senior team with flat hierarchy
Flexible working hours and the option to work partially from home
Working in an English-speaking, international team of HPC experts
Professional development, conference attendance and real influence on our roadmap

Postdoctoral Researcher (f/m/d) in Applied Microeconomics (E13 TV-L, 100%)
Postdoctoral Researcher (f/m/d) in Applied Microeconomics (E13 TV-L, 100%)
The Chair of Machine Learning Methods in Business and Economics at the Faculty of Economics and Social Sciences at the University of Tübingen and the Cluster of Excellence “Machine Learning in Science” invite applications for the position of a
Postdoctoral Researcher (f/m/d) in Applied Microeconomics (E13 TV-L, 100%)
Intended starting date is May 1, 2027, but an earlier or later date is possible. The contract duration is for three years. An extension may be possible.
Our research
The research of our newly formed team spans labor, development, and political economy using both microeconometric methods and field experiments. Beyond this, we aim to develop and apply machine learning methods to both accurately identify economic effects and draw insights from unconventional or unstructured data (e.g., image, video, text, audio).
The position
The post-doctoral position is a full-time research position without a regular teaching load. The successful candidate will be engaged in and take the lead in joint research projects in addition to continuing work on his/her own projects.
We offer:
A lively, internationally oriented, collaborative, and friendly research environment
High-quality research seminar series in economics, machine learning, and management studies
Funding to finance conference trips, hire student research assistants, run experiments, invite coauthors etc.
Your profile
We are seeking a highly motivated, curiosity-driven candidate that holds (or is about to finish) a Ph.D. in economics or a related discipline (e.g., data science). The candidate should have a keen interest and strong background in applied microeconomics (especially labor, political economy or development) as well as a strong interest in exploring unstructured data (e.g. image, text, video,…) using machine learning methods. Candidates with experience in field experiments are particularly encouraged to apply. Fluency in English is indispensable. German skills are not necessary.
Environment
The University of Tübingen, spread across the small and picturesque town of Tübingen in south-west Germany, is one of 11 Universities of Excellence in Germany, with a Cluster of Excellence for Machine Learning in Science. The University’s School of Business and Economics hosts more than 40 faculty, including professors, junior professors, and post docs. It attracts excellent students in its B.Sc. and M.Sc. programs, in part due to its strong international orientation. We offer an open and supportive work environment with a strong research orientation, which is also part of our teaching philosophy.
The University of Tübingen aims to increase the proportion of women in research and teaching and therefore particularly encourages qualified women scientists to apply. The University of Tübingen is committed to equal opportunities and diversity and actively promotes gender equality. Disabled candidates will be given preference over other equally qualified applicants.
How to apply
Please submit your application including a cover letter, job market paper, CV, and at least two letters of recommendation via econjobmarket.org/positions/12911 by 15 November 2026 . In case you have questions about the application process or the position, please contact Prof. Felix Rusche.
The employment will be arranged by the Central Administration of the University of Tübingen.
