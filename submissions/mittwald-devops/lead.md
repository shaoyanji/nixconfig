## What the role actually is

Mittwald is building two new platform teams for its Kubernetes-based hosting platform on **bare metal**.
The stack is Go, Linux, Kubernetes and Helm; the work is operating the hosting system environment,
extending the platform, and writing automation that keeps thousands of instances stable. The advert is
explicit that they train the stack: *"Du musst kein fertiger Profi sein. Wir machen dich fit."*

## Gaps, honestly

- **Kubernetes and Helm are the product**; the candidate has never run a cluster. The advert's

- Go matches; Linux matches strongly; bare-metal operations are adjacent to a thirteen-host fleet.
- Application process is **not CV-first**: a few free-text questions, then a small take-home challenge.
- Remote is **not** promised for this role (other Mittwald roles say hybrid), and Espelkamp is ~700 km
  from Gutach im Breisgau.
